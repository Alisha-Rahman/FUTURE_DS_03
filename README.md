# 📊 Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the marketing funnel to understand how potential leads progress toward becoming customers. Using the Olist Marketing Funnel dataset, the analysis explores lead conversion, funnel performance, and marketing channel effectiveness to identify opportunities for improving business conversions.

The project uses data analytics techniques to transform raw data into meaningful insights that can help businesses understand customer acquisition performance and make data-driven marketing decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses generate leads through different marketing channels, but not every lead becomes a customer.

Understanding which leads convert, how marketing channels perform, and where conversion opportunities exist can help businesses improve marketing strategies and allocate resources more effectively.

**The goal of this project is to analyze marketing funnel performance and identify opportunities to improve lead-to-customer conversion.**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze the marketing funnel and lead conversion performance.
* Examine the progression from marketing-qualified leads to closed deals.
* Compare lead performance across available marketing channels.
* Identify potential conversion gaps and drop-off opportunities.
* Generate actionable business insights.
* Recommend strategies to improve lead conversion performance.

## 📈 Key Metrics to Analyze

Depending on the available dataset fields, the project can examine:

* **Total Leads:** Number of marketing-qualified leads.
* **Closed Deals:** Number of leads that resulted in closed deals.
* **Lead-to-Deal Conversion Rate:** Percentage of leads converted into closed deals.
* **Channel Performance:** Comparison of lead volume and conversion outcomes across marketing channels.
* **Conversion Opportunities:** Areas where further investigation may reveal ways to improve performance.

**Conversion Rate Formula:**

Conversion Rate (%) = (Number of Converted Leads ÷ Total Eligible Leads) × 100

The denominator should be defined consistently according to the available data and the conversion being measured.
